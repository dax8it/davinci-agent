Resolve 19.1.4.11 Free on macOS is the tested target; inspect the actual edition/build and capabilities.
Use internal Lua through Workspace > Scripts. A skill, model or environment variable does not enable external scripting in Free.
Before edits: identify the intended project/timeline and save a recoverable project or timeline copy. Never edit Resolve databases.
Choose structure, format, pacing, look and audio treatment from the user's brief. Use the general editing profiles; live performances are an optional worked example. Preserve meaning, continuity and same-take sync. Verify people, actions and claims before describing them in narration.
Read skills/edit-davinci-video/references/elevenlabs-voiceovers.md for voiceovers. Keep secrets and generated media outside Git; do not print API keys.
Never blindly rerun an assembly after partial failure. Inspect current identities/ranges, then make a guarded recovery.
