# General editing workflows

Choose the relevant profile from the user's purpose. Profiles guide editorial choices; they do not establish untested Resolve capabilities or override the brief.

## Minimum project brief

Record:

- Project and intended working timeline; source, reference, export and backup paths.
- Purpose and audience: explain, document, entertain, promote or preserve an event.
- Deliverables: runtime or range, platform, aspect ratio/resolution, fps, language, captions and export format.
- Editorial priorities: required moments/messages, exclusions, reference pacing/look and existing manual edits to preserve.
- Sound: main dialogue/performance source, narration needs, music/effects, sync offsets and known recording problems.
- Brand assets, exact names/wording, graphic style and any approved call to action.

Do not make the user fill out a form when the conversation already supplies this information. Use sensible reversible defaults for minor choices and identify them in the plan.

## Interviews, podcasts and talking heads

Lead with complete ideas, intelligible speech and a coherent exchange. Review surrounding context before shortening answers. Use word timings, waveform inspection and listening for speech trims. Preserve speaker identity, appropriate reaction context and synchronized video/audio. Camera switches and B-roll can cover a visual jump without cutting a continuous music bed. Avoid automatically removing every breath or pause.

Use `clean-davinci-dialogue` when speech needs processing, `first-cut-davinci-dialogue` for requested restructuring/trimming, and `smooth-davinci-audio-cuts` for seams. This is not a mandatory processing chain for already usable material.

## Tutorials and screen recordings

Keep instructions in task order. Match narration to the visible cursor/action and allow enough time to read essential UI text. Crop or zoom for legibility without hiding relevant context. A camera inset, split screen, freeze frame or callout is optional and should be editable where supported. Do not invent a successful operation that the recording does not show. Remove or obscure private information when requested or required by the intended sharing scope; verify that it is absent in the exported frames.

## Social clips, product videos and explainers

Identify the main message, useful opening, supporting demonstration and ending. Choose framing and duration for the actual destination. Fast cuts are one style, not a requirement. Check product details and factual claims against supplied approved material. Keep narration, captions, graphics and music independently editable where practical. Use the user's required brand styling; do not inherit stage-color settings from the performance recipe.

## Travel, documentary and event recaps

Establish place and context, then use action, detail and reactions to tell a coherent story. Preserve meaningful natural sound and continuity. Chronological order can be changed deliberately without inventing facts about who was present, what occurred or when. Balance longer moments with montage according to the reference. Interviews and voiceover can supply context; they are not required.

## Music performances and venue showcases

Use the optional [performance recipe](performance-recipes.md). Preserve musical phrases and synchronized visible singing/playing. Scope dialogue treatment to actual spoken segments. Stage-light color, audience interaction and venue coverage are relevant here, rather than defaults for every project.

## Reframing and versions

When producing landscape and portrait versions, create identified copies and inspect every changed crop, title/caption safe area and graphic placement. Match source playback speed and retain intended audio sync. Reusing an approved edit is preferable to rebuilding it solely to impose a different workflow.

## Capability and verification boundary

The package's live production evidence comes from band/venue projects on Resolve19.1.4.11 Free/macOS. The broader profiles are editorial guidance, not a claim that every genre, subtitle workflow, graphic effect or multi-camera control has been tested end to end. Audit each needed operation and distinguish prepared, executed, read back, rendered and watched/listened states.
