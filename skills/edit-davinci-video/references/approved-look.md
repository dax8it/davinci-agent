# Performance and venue visual reference

This profile replaces upstream talking-head/screen-recording examples. The user's reference and project brief determine the look; this filename does not mean every choice below is already approved.

- Balance real establishing views, performer close-ups, instrument details, band interaction, crowd energy and room atmosphere according to the reference.
- Preserve chosen continuous music across picture coverage. Musical phrases, gestures, vocal delivery and instrumental focus guide changes; camera changes do not imply music cuts.
- For vertical delivery, compose around the active performer and instrument. Inspect heads, hands, signs, and stage edges rather than assuming one centered crop works for every shot.
- Match cameras while retaining intentional blue, purple, red and amber stage lighting. Avoid blanket neutralization or crushing all room detail for contrast.
- Keep identity, venue details, song labels and closing information editable. Verify current names and contact details rather than copying prior event details from a reference.
- Use restrained transitions unless the reference supports a visible effect. Inspect dissolves and animation in motion. Sampled frames cannot prove motion, precise transition duration, beat alignment or sound.
- Scope speech treatment to speech. Singing, sustained notes, applause and room tails are performance content.

For each reference, record measured duration, aspect ratio, fps, audio format, observed opening/middle/ending, shot variety, titles and review limitations in the project brief. Cut-detector timestamps are candidates, especially under flashing stage lights. Reuse editorial intent rather than forcing the new event to the reference's exact duration or fps.
