# General editing prompts

Replace placeholders with the actual brief and paths. These examples do not automatically authorize edits to an open project.

## Inspect any project

> Use edit-davinci-video and the Resolve19 profile. Read the project brief and run the read-only audit for [project]. Identify sources, timeline settings, audio roles and available tools. Report missing capabilities without editing the timeline yet.

## Interview or podcast

> Make a [length] edit from [sources] for [audience/platform]. Keep the strongest complete ideas and preserve each speaker's meaning. Tighten repetition within that scope, use synchronized camera changes and relevant B-roll, and keep dialogue processing editable. Match [reference]. Save a working copy and export to [path].

## Tutorial or screen recording

> Turn this screen recording and narration into a clear tutorial about [task]. Keep steps in order, show each action while it is explained, make essential UI text readable and add editable step labels. Use [format], with a camera inset only where it helps. Check the actual recording before claiming a step succeeded.

## Product or social video

> Create a [duration/format] video for [product/topic] using these assets and approved copy. Follow [reference] for pacing, color and graphics. Show the important details, use the supplied brand assets, and include [ending/call to action]. Add narration/captions only as specified. Preserve the original project and render a new version.

## Travel or event recap

> Create a recap from [sources], with an opening that establishes the place, key activities, detail shots and a clear ending. Keep meaningful natural sound and match [reference]. Use [duration/aspect/fps], avoid unsupported statements about the people or event, and export to [path].

## Narrated explainer

> Use the ElevenLabs reference to generate short conversational narration beats for this approved script. Align each beat with the correct visual, preserve natural speaking speed and let background audio recover in gaps. Keep narration and background sound on separate tracks, then check intelligibility and timing in the actual export.

## Revise an approved edit

> In a new version of [timeline], change only [specified section/text/look/audio]. Preserve the remaining source ranges, framing, effects and sound. Save a backup, verify the change and give me the exact exported filename and directory.
